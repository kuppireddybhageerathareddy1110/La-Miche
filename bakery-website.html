Our Heritage
La Miche

Flour, Water,
Salt & Time

In 1987, Marie Delacroix set up her first wood-fired oven in a tiny Pondicherry kitchen. Three decades on, La Miche is still family-run — and still using Marie's original sourdough starter, lovingly fed every day.

We source ancient grain flours from small Indian mills, churn our own cultured butter, and let time do the heavy lifting. No shortcuts, no additives, no compromises.

How We Bake

From Grain to Table

01

Source

We partner with heritage grain farmers across Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu, milling fresh each week.

02

Mix & Ferment

Long cold fermentation — 18 to 36 hours — develops deep flavour and natural leavening.

03

Shape & Proof

Hand-shaped by our bakers at 4 am, every loaf is given time to rise on its own terms.

04

Fire-Bake

Our stone deck ovens hit 260°C — yielding that unmistakable crust and open crumb.
