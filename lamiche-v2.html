Heritage
Our Heritage

Flour, Water,
Salt & Time

In 1987, Marie Delacroix set up her first wood-fired oven in a tiny Pondicherry kitchen. Three decades on, La Miche is still family-run — and still using Marie's original sourdough starter, lovingly fed every morning.

We source heirloom grain flours from small Indian mills, churn our own cultured butter, and let slow fermentation do the heavy lifting. No shortcuts, no additives, no compromises — ever.
